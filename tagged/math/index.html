Bianchi Eisenstein series I
Posted on 2026-11-03

Let F be an imaginary quadratic field, with ring of integers \OO_F. The group \Gamma = \PSL_2(\OO_F) is a Bianchi group. For simplicity, we will assume that the class number of F is 1.The main difference for larger class number is that the tuples of functions we will describe need to replaced with h_F-many tuples, and their Fourier expansions change. We will describe some Eisenstein series for \Gamma.

First, a brief recap on Bianchi modular forms. The group \SL_2(\C) acts on \HH_3 = \C \times \R_{>0}, the upper half-space, via the following formula:

\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d\end{bmatrix} \cdot (z,t) = \left( \frac{(az+b)(\overline{cz+d}) +a \overline{c} t^2}{\abs{cz+d}^2 + \abs{ct}^2}, \frac{t}{\abs{cz+d}^2 + \abs{ct}^2}\right).
